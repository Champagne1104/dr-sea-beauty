Pricing method

What “verified price” means

A product becomes price-ready only when an official regular consumer price can be matched to the same reference, name and size. The Dr. SEA Beauty price is then that regular price minus $0.50.

The available order workbook is clearly labeled EXW Price (USD). Those wholesale amounts are not used to calculate customer-facing prices.
